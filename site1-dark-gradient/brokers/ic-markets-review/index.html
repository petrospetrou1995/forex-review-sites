What to verify before choosing IC Markets

Broker conditions can vary by legal entity, country of residence, instrument and account type. The review's displayed rating is an editorial assessment, not a customer-rating aggregate or evidence of a standardized live-account execution test. Confirm the following directly before opening an account.

  • Legal entity and oversight: Identify the contracting entity in the broker's legal documents, then cross-check that entity and its permissions with the relevant official financial-regulator register. Do not assume that all group entities offer identical investor protections.
  • Real trading costs: Compare typical spreads, per-side commissions, financing/swaps, currency conversion and inactivity charges for the specific account and instrument. Promotional minimum spreads are not average execution costs.
  • Deposits and withdrawals: Check supported methods, third-party payment rules, minimums, fees, processing times and country restrictions in the broker's current terms.
  • Risk and fit: Check client-money treatment, negative balance protection where applicable, leverage limitations and whether the product is appropriate for your jurisdiction.

Independent verification: FCA Register · CySEC register · ASIC Professional Registers. These links are verification starting points, not claims that IC Markets is authorized by each regulator.

Verify the IC Markets legal entity

CySEC lists IC Markets (EU) Ltd under licence 362/18, and identifies icmarkets.eu and ic.com/eu as approved domains. This registration applies to the named EU entity, not automatically to every IC Markets-branded service or to residents of every country.

Verify IC Markets (EU) Ltd directly in the CySEC register. Confirm which entity would hold your account before depositing.
