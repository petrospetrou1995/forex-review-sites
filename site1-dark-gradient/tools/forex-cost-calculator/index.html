How the estimate works

Spread cost = spread in pips × pip value per lot × lots. Round-trip commission = commission per lot per side × lots × 2. Net financing cost = −signed swap per night × nights (positive swap is a credit). Total = spread + commission + net financing + other fees. This model assumes constant spreads and pip values, proportional commissions, and no slippage, conversion changes, or tiered swap schedules. Negative totals represent net credits, not guaranteed trading profit.
