# Design, SEO, and AI-search growth audit — 2026-10-26

## Scope and important limitations
Source: GitHub repository plus public pages https://brokerproreviews.com/, /brokers/libertex-review/, /methodology/. No Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics, or real-device Lighthouse measurements were available; no ranking, CTR, conversion or AI referral uplift is claimed.

## Highest priorities

1. **Trust and editorial evidence (P0)**: The Libertex page labels 4.9/5 as "internal score for comparison + schema", and the main page displays rating aggregates. Never use fabricated user review counts or unsupported aggregate ratings. Document category weights, reviewer names and expertise, tests performed, records/sources and date last verified; review schema types and eligibility before deploying.
2. **Actual valuable content (P1)**: Review pages repeat broad, non-specific language and have few sourced first-hand measurements. Replace generic copy with account/entity/country-specific regulatory facts, account-type costs, dated example spreads/commission, deposits/withdrawals, testing methods and traceable evidence. Clearly separate editorial scoring from user ratings.
3. **Homepage design hierarchy (P1)**: Make the primary action "Compare brokers" obvious with filters for resident country, regulatory entity, platform and cost. Move sprawling news listings below broker selection; use compact, accessible comparison summaries and editorial notes. Test mobile CLS/LCP/INP rather than assuming visual improvements increase traffic.
4. **Country targeting (P1)**: Build genuine Mexico/Colombia and other regional pages only where independently verified local payment methods, entities and regulation can be supported. Avoid location-keyword swaps or duplicate template pages.
5. **Indexable translation strategy (P1)**: Browser-only Spanish switching does not give a separate Spanish URL. Serve fully translated /es/ pages with reciprocal hreflang, correct html lang and self canonicals after translations are ready.
6. **Canonical hygiene (P1)**: Site1 custom-domain robots and sitemap must agree. Site2 preview-domain configuration requires validation against actual production host. Avoid duplicate domains and indexable pages that canonicalize elsewhere.
7. **Dates/news automation (P1)**: Limit low-quality near-duplicate daily news archive pages; use editorially valuable context, source URLs, publication dates and corrections. Ensure dates are real and linked article sources are not misrepresented as first-party reporting.
8. **Accessibility and design (P2)**: Test keyboard navigation, menu focus, contrast, tables on narrow devices, font loading, tap targets and reduced motion. Use consent-aware analytics where needed.
9. **Search/AI appearance (P1)**: Ensure content is indexable, text-visible, internally linked and supported with clear citations, headings, factual tables, original comparisons and stable canonical URLs. Google's official guidance says no special AI-specific schema or llms.txt is required for Google AI Overviews/AI Mode.
10. **Measurement (P1)**: Configure Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance, GA4 conversions, server crawl logs. Track indexed pages, queries, impressions, CTR, conversions, organic traffic and AI citations/referrals. Record baselines before redesign.

## Suggested conversion-oriented page layout
Header > clear value proposition > country/entity selector > 3–5 reviewed options with last-verified date > comparison table > transparent methodology teaser > detailed broker content > expert author/source notes > newsletter/news > footer legal/affiliate disclosures.

## Editorial content to prioritize
- Cyprus/EU vs LATAM broker entity and leverage differences explained with dated regulator references.
- All-in EUR/USD cost comparison with commission and swaps for standardized trade example.
- Broker-specific withdrawal and support tests only when actually performed.
- Independently sourced broker entity tables updated on a recorded schedule.
- Helpful educational explainers with author credentials and practical worked examples.
